Lacanau Shoreline Evolution Model — BRGM
Coastal Risk Engineer · Nov 2022 – Present
Built and validated the coupled LX-ST model (cross-shore + longshore sediment transport) and applied it to the 5 km Lacanau coastline, running SSP2-4.5, SSP5-8.5, and High-End scenarios to 2100 with sensitivity testing. Published in Environmental Modelling & Software (2025).

ShoreTrans: Research Code to Engineering Software — BRGM
Internship · Feb 2022 – Aug 2022
Turned the published ShoreTrans model (MATLAB) into fast, portable Fortran software, adding 2D capability for complex coastal geometries. Open-sourced and validated against observed coastal-evolution data.
github.com/mtraboulsi689/ShoreTrans-fortran
Gironde Estuary Climate Impact — Cerema (ECCLIPSE Project)
Internship · Mar 2021 – Sep 2021
